ELIZABETHTOWN CITY COUNCIL
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
SEPTEMBER 14, 2026

The Elizabethtown City Council met in a Special Session at 04:30 PM with Mayor Jeffrey H. Gregory presiding. The meeting was held at City Council Chambers, 212 West Dixie Avenue, Elizabethtown, Kentucky.

PRESENT: Tim Isaacs, Lamar Jones, Julia Springsteen, and Mika Tyler
ABSENT: Tony Bishop and Marty Fulkerson

1. CALL TO ORDER: The meeting was called to order by Mayor Jeffrey H. Gregory.
2. INVOCATION: Invocation was given by Council Member Mika Tyler
3. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Mayor Gregory led the Pledge of Allegiance.
4. ROLL CALL: City Clerk Graham took a roll call of the City Council Members. Four of the six members were present, constituting a legal quorum.
5. PROCEDURE FOR PUBLIC COMMENTS: Mayor Gregory announced if anyone wishes to speak during the 'Public Comments' section of the meeting, they should sign in at the side desk with staff.
6. APPROVAL OF MINUTES- August 17, 2026: Minutes of the Regular Meeting held on August 17, 2026, were previously distributed to each Council Member for consideration. There being no corrections or amendments thereto, it was moved by Council Member Springsteen, seconded by Council Member Isaacs, and carried to adopt the minutes as written. Council Members Isaacs, Jones, Springsteen, and Tyler voted aye. Motion passed 4-0.
