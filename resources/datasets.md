# Datasets — open government & AI

Publicly available datasets relevant to digital government and AI. Prefer linking to canonical sources; note license and update cadence.

## U.S. federal

- [data.gov](https://data.gov) — U.S. government's open data portal (thousands of datasets across sectors)
- [OMB Federal AI Use Case Inventory (2025)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/GreatNorthCollective/greatnorth-us-federal-ai-inventory) — 3,611 agency AI use cases, normalized
- [Google Civic Information API](https://developers.google.com/civic-information) — polling places, representatives, elections

## International / comparative

- [OECD Digital Government Index & OURdata Index (2025)](https://www.oecd.org/digital/digital-government/) — benchmarks of digital transformation and open data policies across countries
- [Open Corporates](https://opencorporates.com) — open database of companies worldwide

## Local / municipal

- Socrata open-data portals (559+ via civic-ai-tools) — city-level data on 311, permits, budgets, etc.
- State and local open data portals (varies by jurisdiction)

## Notes

- Always record the source URL, retrieval date, and license in `data/` alongside any mirrored files.
- Large raw files should live outside git (Git LFS or external storage); keep metadata and samples here.
